Inside the story

FORT WAYNE, INDIANA • NIGHT ROTATION

THE REPORT SAYS ONE VEHICLE.

THE DAMAGE SAYS THERE WAS ANOTHER.

THE DRIVER IS GONE.

Tow operator Jess trusts boring photographs. They settle old dents, protect every handoff, and keep a damaged vehicle's story from changing after it enters Ridgeway's gate.

Then an abandoned green Pontiac arrives from a guardrail crash carrying red paint and an amber fragment that do not belong. A battered red Ranger soon enters the yard with matching damage—and a driver who cannot remember the impact.

The cars may prove contact. They cannot name who drove. As police questions turn observation into suspicion, Jess must learn where evidence ends before one family's uncertainty becomes an accusation.

Metal records the impact. It does not name the hands on the wheel.
