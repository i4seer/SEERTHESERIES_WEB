Inside the story

SPRINGFIELD, OHIO • MARCH 2002

THE HOUSE IS SIX FEET WIDER.

THE WALL HAS NO DOOR.

THE OWNER WANTS IT LEFT ALONE.

Contractor Evan Holt begins every job outside, where brick keeps a straighter account than old plaster. At 314 Linden, his measurements expose nearly six feet of house that no interior room contains.

Homeowner Margaret Vale already knows the narrow room is there. She will pay more to route the work around it, and her instruction is clear: do not open the sealed wall—or find another way inside.

When curiosity turns professional access into trespass, Evan damages more than paint. Then a hidden leak forces the wall open for legitimate work, and every measurement must answer a harder question: what does the project need, and what still belongs only to Margaret?

A question can be real without becoming permission.
