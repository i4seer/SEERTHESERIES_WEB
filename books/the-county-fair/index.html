MICHIGAN • ONE SUMMER

THE FARM EXPECTS HIM BACK.

THE MIDWAY IS READY TO LEAVE.

SCOTT HAS ONE SUMMER TO CHOOSE.

At eighteen, Scott Dwyer's future has already been decided: stay on the family farm and take his place beside his father.

Then a temporary job with a traveling midway puts him under the rides, beside the people who keep them running, and close to Anne Bell—a girl building plans of her own.

By summer's end, Scott has three roads in front of him: the farm that raised him, the crew that made room for him, and a railroad shop where he could begin again. Choosing one means leaving the others.

Leaving is easy. Choosing is the hard part.
