Inside the story

BRIAR COUNTY COURTHOUSE • COURTROOM TWO

THE CABINET IS LOCKED.

THE ACCESS LOG IS BLANK.

EXHIBIT 27 KEEPS MOVING.

Leah Carden keeps Courtroom Two running by making sure every exhibit, key, and signature is exactly where it belongs. Then a red ledger moves inside a locked cabinet—and no one has signed the access sheet.

Instead of reporting it, Leah watches. She keeps a private log. She sets a harmless test. By the time two missing pages surface, her careful search has become part of a discovery failure that could destroy a murder trial.

With a defendant's freedom, a widow's answers, and Leah's own career at stake, the court must decide whether good intentions can repair a record that no longer deserves trust.

What isn't recorded can still change everything.
