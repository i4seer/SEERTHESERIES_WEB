Inside the story

MONTGOMERY COUNTY, OHIO • SALE WEEK

THE FAMILY KNOWS WHAT TO SELL.

SOMEONE ELSE'S LIFE IS STILL INSIDE.

THE DOORS OPEN THURSDAY.

Nora Bennett knows how to divide a stranger's home: sell, return, donate, discard. At Eleanor Redding's estate, two worn chairs and a second set of clothes point to something no checklist covers.

Ruth Ames lived here. Her mail arrived here. Her sewing machine waits upstairs. But the family hired Nora to finish the sale, and every delay costs time. When Nora starts protecting Ruth's belongings, careful observation becomes an excuse to cross a boundary.

With buyers waiting at the door, Nora must separate ownership from use, evidence from inference—and decide whether being right can ever excuse acting without permission.

Seeing the truth does not make it yours.
