Inside the story

BARLOW INDUSTRIAL PRODUCTS • 1992

THREE MACHINES WERE PAID FOR.

NOT ONE REACHED THE FLOOR.

ONE MAN KEPT DRAWING PAY.

After thirty-four years at Barlow Industrial Products, Walter Quinn knows every machine on the floor—or thinks he does.

As the failing factory is tagged for auction, three expensive machining centers appear in the records. No one remembers receiving them. There are no foundations, freight bills, or rigging scars. Then Walter finds a second impossibility: Earl Morrow, who supposedly quit in 1983, remained on the payroll for seven years.

Following invoices, lender files, and a receiving ledger in Earl's block letters, Walter uncovers the bargain that kept the plant alive—and asks what an honest man owes the truth when the lie paid everyone's wages.

The truth begins with what never arrived.
