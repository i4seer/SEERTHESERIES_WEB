Inside the story

SUGAR ISLAND, MICHIGAN • 1929

TWENTY-FOUR CASES WENT OUT.

TWENTY-THREE CAME BACK.

JACK HART DID NOT.

Eighteen-year-old Will Hart knows boats, engines, and the night crossings his family uses to carry Canadian whiskey past U.S. Prohibition.

When one case disappears and Uncle Jack vanishes with it, Will follows fuel marks, secret records, and quiet patrols into a protection scheme his father refuses to explain.

But the missing case and the missing man are not the same mystery—and the truth will cost Will more than he expects to pay.

Some loads have to be counted twice.
