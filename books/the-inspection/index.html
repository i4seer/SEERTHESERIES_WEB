Inside the story

THE BENTON APARTMENTS • 1996

TWENTY-FOUR UNITS ON PAPER.

A DOOR MARKED TWENTY-FIVE.

A WOMAN WHO HAS LIVED THERE FOR DECADES.

A junior building inspector is sent to document the failing Benton Apartments before demolition. The records show twenty-four units. On the third floor, beyond the last approved door, is Apartment 25.

Eleanor Pike has lived there for decades. Her neighbors know her, her rent has been paid, and her key fits. The city has no record of her home—and the measurements say there is no room for it.

As demolition closes in, the inspector must decide what can be proved, what can be made safe, and what the city owes a woman whose life exists everywhere except the plan.

A person matters more than the plan.
