About Seer Stories

A closer look.

Different lives. Different places. A complete story in every book.

The collection

People who know their work.
Questions they can’t leave alone.

Seer Stories brings together B. Carter’s standalone novels. A river runner counts a missing load. A locksmith recognizes a familiar method. A clerk notices a record that should not exist.

The settings and genres change. What carries across the collection is attention: to a place, a trade, a family, or the moment when knowing something becomes a responsibility.

Historical fiction, coming-of-age stories, mysteries, suspense, and quiet horror all have a place here. Every book introduces its own cast and closes its own arc. Begin with whichever story speaks to you.

A note from B. Carter

About the author

I write the kind of books I enjoy — full of wonder, big questions, and just enough answers to keep the pages turning.

When I’m not writing, I’m usually reading, walking, or staring at the sky, letting a new idea take shape.

I live with too many books, not enough shelves, and a strong policy of leaving the hall light on.

Thanks for joining the journey. There’s always more to see.

B. Carter
